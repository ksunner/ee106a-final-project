# Ladder Checkmate Example
# This demonstrates the classic two-rook checkmate pattern
#
# Initial position (simplified endgame):
# - White: King on b4, Rooks on a4 and b4
# - Black: King on g5
#
# Format: from_square-to_square (one move per line)
# Lines starting with # are comments

# Move 1: White plays Rg5 (Rook from g4 to g5)
g4-g5

# Move 1: Black plays Kg6 (King from g5 to g6)
e5-e6

# Move 2: White plays Ra6+ (Rook from a4 to a6, giving check)
a4-a6

# Move 2: Black plays Kf7 (King from g6 to f7, escaping check)
e6-e7

# Move 3: White plays Rb7+ (Rook from g5 to b7, giving check)
g5-g7

# Move 3: Black plays Ke8 (King from f7 to e8, forced to edge)
e7-e8

# Move 4: White plays Ra8# (Rook from a6 to a8, checkmate!)
a6-a8
